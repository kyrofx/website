# .cat implementation and registration policy

## Sources reviewed

- The user-supplied **.CAT DOMAIN POLICIES — Terms and Conditions** (Accent Obert / Fundació puntCAT), especially sections 2.2, 4, 8, and 9.
- The registry's [English FAQ](https://domini.cat/en/faqs), accessed during this revision. Under “Can anyone register a .cat domain?” it states: “You do not need to be Catalan to have a .cat; the only requirement is that the website associated with your domain has meaningful content in Catalan or promotes the Catalan language or culture.”
- Section 2.2 incorporates Specification 12 of the [ICANN agreement](https://www.icann.org/sites/default/files/tlds/cat/cat-agmt-html-08oct15-en.htm). That linked document returned HTTP errors during this review; its additional text has not been independently verified.

## Website implementation

The entire small portfolio has equivalent English and Catalan pages. The Catalan version includes the actual profile facts, project navigation and filtering, contact labels, copy feedback, and accessible names. Repository names, technologies, and contact addresses remain their proper names. The same concise content rules apply in both languages: no taglines, descriptive blurbs, or compliance banner.

| English | Catalan |
| --- | --- |
| `/` | `/ca/` |
| `/projects.html` | `/ca/projects.html` |
| `/about.html` | `/ca/about.html` |
| `/contact.html` | `/ca/contact.html` |

The EN / CA switch always opens the corresponding translation. Catalan pages render without JavaScript. Each has `lang="ca"`, reciprocal `hreflang` links, a canonical URL under `https://kyro.cat`, and inclusion in the sitemap. Search engines can reach the content normally. Keep both versions synchronized and publicly reachable when deploying.

This implements a real Catalan version of the site. The reviewed FAQ gives no minimum word count; final eligibility remains subject to the registry's assessment and all registration obligations.

## Registrant obligations

These are handled through the registrar and are not public portfolio copy:

- **Section 2.2:** the registrant declares participation in the Catalan linguistic and cultural community under the incorporated agreement.
- **Section 4:** supply accurate real registration details, intended use, and whether the registrant is a natural or legal person; keep them current. The policy contains restrictions on anonymization/interposition services. Review these with the registrar rather than publishing private registration data on the site.
- **Sections 8 and 9:** respond to correction or breach notices within the stated 15-calendar-day periods and use the domain consistently with its declared purpose and the applicable policies.

## Six-month timing

The user reported a six-month requirement. Neither the pasted policy nor the reviewed FAQ states that deadline. This does not invalidate a separate registrar condition. Keep the registrar's stated deadline, and confirm its start date with the registrar. No registration date was supplied, so no calendar deadline is inferred here.

The branch prepares the website content. Publishing it, maintaining the Catalan version, and meeting the registration obligations are separate steps; this document is not a certification of compliance.
